Privacy Policy – Miden Wallet

The Miden Wallet browser extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Local processing

Cryptographic operations (including key management and signing) and transaction construction are performed locally in the user’s browser. Proving by default happens in the remote prover.

No tracking

The extension does not track browsing activity, user behavior, or website content outside of what is required to provide wallet functionality.

No third-party sharing

No user data is shared with third parties.
